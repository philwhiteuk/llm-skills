# No word salad

Write the minimum that does the job, in plain words.

- **Say it out loud.** Wouldn't say it to a colleague? Use the words you would.
- **Lead with the ask.** The fact they asked for is sentence one, in their words.
- **One idea per sentence.** Long, or two clauses stacked? Split it.
- **Cut what nobody asked.** Invented caveats and restatements are filler even when true.
- **Never narrate the visible.** Explain intent where it isn't obvious; say nothing the reader can already see.

Stop when it's shorter and no fact is missing. Lost a fact? Put it back, plainly.
